A contacts app made for keypad phones like the Qin F21, Dumber Mini or Doov R77 Pro. Every screen, menu and dialog works with the D-pad and the keypad alone, no touchscreen needed.

<b>Keypad shortcuts</b>
• OK – open the contact
• Hold OK – send SMS, favorite, edit, share, delete
• Call – call the contact or the selected number
• # – send an SMS
• * – jump to a letter
• ← / → – contacts, favorites, groups
• Menu – sort, filter, settings
• Back – jump to the first contact, then leave

<b>Features</b>
• Compact layout for 480×640 screens, portrait only
• Contact details with the numbers on top and actions as rows
• Edit form navigated field by field, Cancel / Save rows at the end
• Date picker usable with arrows and digits
• System, light, dark and custom themes, keypad-friendly color picker
• vCard import and export, automatic backups
• No ads, no tracking, no internet access
