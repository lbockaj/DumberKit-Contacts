A contacts app for keypad phones like the Qin F21, Dumber Mini or Doov R77 Pro. Every screen, menu and dialog works with the D-pad and the keypad alone, on a screen as small as 480×640. Touch is switched off inside the app, so nothing gets pressed by accident in your pocket.

<b>Keys</b>
• OK – open the contact, or pick the selected item
• Hold OK – what you can do with the contact: SMS, favorite, edit, share, delete
• Call – call the contact or the selected number
• # – send an SMS
• * or Ab – jump to a letter in an A–Z grid, also with the digit keys
• ← / → – switch between Contacts, Favorites and Groups
• Menu – what you can do on the current screen
• Back – go back; on the list it first jumps to the first contact

<b>Features</b>
• Search from the magnifier, ready for typing with Traditional T9
• Contact details with the numbers on top and the actions as rows
• Edit form walked through field by field, Cancel / Save rows at the end, dates typed with the digit keys
• Favorites and groups with their own keypad menus: add members, SMS or e-mail the whole group, ringtone
• System, light, dark and custom themes, a keypad-friendly color picker, red or any other icon color
• vCard import and export, automatic backups
• No ads, no tracking, no internet access
• Translated into over 40 languages
